---
name: Brand-location mapping
description: The Social Group’s official sites list multiple distinct brands at some of the same retail destinations.
---

The location directory must treat each brand-and-outlet combination as its own record. A shared destination such as 163 Retail Park can have separate The Social, Lisette’s, and Cafe Deli experiences, with different addresses, menus, hours, booking destinations, and ordering destinations.

**Why:** Auditing the official sites showed that collapsing a mall into one outlet hides real customer choices and sends people to the wrong brand.

**How to apply:** When importing or updating official location content, reconcile brand, outlet, hours, address, menus, booking, ordering, directions, and social links together rather than deduplicating only by mall name.
