# THE SOCIAL GROUP — WEBSITE & DIGITAL GROWTH PLATFORM

## MASTER PRODUCT + DESIGN + ENGINEERING BRIEF

You are building a new website from scratch for **The Social Group of Restaurants, Malaysia**.

This is not a simple restaurant website and it is not a corporate brochure.

The objective is to create a **high-end hospitality website that functions as a digital discovery, SEO, local-search, conversion and first-party customer acquisition platform**.

The finished product must look premium and distinctive, but its underlying architecture must be designed around measurable business growth.

---

# 1. COMPANY

## The Social Group of Restaurants

Malaysia.

Current brands:

### 01 — The Social

Contemporary social dining, food, drinks, neighbourhood atmosphere, gatherings, nightlife/social occasions.

### 02 — Lisette’s Café & Bakery

Café, bakery, wholesome food, lifestyle, seasonal food, quality ingredients, relaxed dining.

### 03 — Cafe Deli by El Mesón

Spanish-influenced casual dining, comfort, all-day dining, approachable food and family/social occasions.

IMPORTANT:

There may be additional brands in the future.

The platform must therefore be **brand-agnostic and extensible**.

Do not architect the application around exactly three brands.

The CMS and frontend must support:

```text
Brand A
Brand B
Brand C
Future Brand D
Future Brand E
...
```

A future brand must be addable primarily through CMS/content configuration without redesigning the application.
